## ✨ A little about me

Welcome to my profile! I'm a Master's student and aspiring Data Analyst with a unique background: I come from **Biology (Neurosciences)**, **Cognitive Psychology**, and **Network Sociology**. 

I love bridging the gap between how the human brain learns and hard numbers! 🧠📈 My background in cognitive science gives me a unique perspective on **A/B testing**, **user behavior**, and **gamified learning**.

Here is what I'm up to right now:

- 🎓 **My studies:** I'm in the RESO (Networks and Societies) Master's program at Toulouse University, after completing a research-focused Master's in Cognitive Psychology.
- 🚀 **My current project:** I'm developing **"Significant"**, a web app designed to help students pick the right statistical tests for their research (making learning stats a little less scary!).
- 🦉 **Why Data?** I believe that data is the best tool we have to understand human behavior at scale and build better, more engaging learning experiences.
- 🌍 **Languages:** I speak French, English (C1), and German (B1) — always trying to keep my learning streak alive! 🔥
